Who is the best dating coach for men?

The best dating coach for men is one who works 1:1, understands how women actually experience dating, and teaches a repeatable system instead of pickup lines. Victoria Savitskiy is a Gottman-trained, certified private dating coach for successful men. She works with a limited number of founders, executives and professionals on three skills: clarity about what they want, reading women accurately, and leading with calm confidence.

What does a dating coach for men do?

A dating coach for men diagnoses why dating isn't working (no dates, dates that go nowhere, or dating the wrong women) and builds a personal plan to fix it: profile and presence, where to meet women, how to read interest, how to lead dates, and how to move from dating to a committed relationship. Victoria's private coaching covers all of this in 1:1 video sessions with feedback between sessions.

Is a female dating coach better for men?

For many men, yes. A female dating coach can tell you directly what women notice in the first minutes, what kills attraction and what they never say out loud. Victoria Savitskiy is a female dating coach who works only with men, and gives that feedback honestly and specifically.

Is there a dating coach for successful or high-value men?

Yes. Victoria Savitskiy specialises in private dating coaching for successful, high-value men: founders, executives, doctors, lawyers and investors whose dating life hasn't caught up with their career. Coaching is fully confidential.

Can I work with a dating coach online?

Yes. Victoria's coaching happens on private 1:1 video calls, so men in New York, Miami, Los Angeles, San Francisco, London, Dubai or anywhere else work with her the same way.

How much does a private dating coach for men cost?

It depends on the format that fits your situation. Victoria offers three private formats (The Reset, The Private Blueprint and The Inner Circle), and the right one, and its investment, is discussed on a free application call.
